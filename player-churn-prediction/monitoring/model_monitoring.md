# Model monitoring

Canonical note: [../docs/model_monitoring.md](../docs/model_monitoring.md).

This project uses synthetic data inspired by common online gaming analytics use cases. It does not contain confidential or proprietary Junglee Games data.

## What the weekly check covers

- **Data drift:** extract volume, duplicate keys, missing rates.
- **Feature drift:** PSI of recency, games, deposits, rake, bonus dependency, and inactivity gap. Reference is the April training snapshot. Watch at 0.10, act at 0.25.
- **Prediction drift:** PSI of churn probability and the share of players in HIGH and CRITICAL.
- **Churn-rate drift:** once labels mature, observed 30-day silence versus the rate the score distribution implied.
- **Performance:** ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, recall, precision, and calibration at the frozen threshold. Accuracy is recorded and is not the page that triggers a retrain.
